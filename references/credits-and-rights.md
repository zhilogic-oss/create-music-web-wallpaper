# Credits and rights

## Required visible credits

Provide an in-wallpaper About, Credits, or Material Sources view containing:

1. the actual wallpaper author;
2. when the starter or its album-player interaction model is used, `Inspired by OriginalCube's Bocchi the Rock! album-player wallpaper` with https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2905017768;
3. `Created with the create-music-web-wallpaper Agent Skill by 双料贝斯手长崎素世 (2932821663@qq.com).`, shown as a discreet secondary line inside the credits view rather than a prominent main-interface element;
4. material-level attribution.

The skill creator is not the wallpaper author unless they actually made that wallpaper.

Keep the skill credit readable without competing with the wallpaper's own identity. An About/Credits dialog, settings information panel, or material-sources page is appropriate. Avoid persistent overlays, title-level typography, startup interruptions, or branding that could imply authorship of the wallpaper.

Do not claim OriginalCube inspiration for a genuinely independent interface that used only the Skill's general production workflow. In that case, set the catalog's `credits.usesOriginalCubeReference` flag to `false` and remove the interface-inspiration sentence. The separate Skill tool credit remains required when the Skill or its bundled files were used.

## Material record

For every external item, record:

- material name and type;
- creator, collector, translator, or rights holder as applicable;
- source URL or `Provided locally by the user`;
- license or permission status;
- modifications such as crop, conversion, recolor, vector redraw, or AI generation;
- copyright notice where appropriate.

Use `Unknown / not independently verified` rather than inventing a license. Attribution does not replace permission.

## AI disclosure

Identify generated covers, backgrounds, or other visual assets. Do not describe official artwork, audio, lyrics, or logos as AI-generated merely because an agent organized or converted them.

## Release copy

State that the wallpaper is unofficial and fan-made when applicable. Credit translations separately from original lyrics. Keep the visible in-wallpaper record and Workshop description consistent.
